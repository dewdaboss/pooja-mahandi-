FAQ

Aksar puche jaane wale sawaal

Mehndi ka rate kitna hota hai?

Design ₹200 se shuru hota hai (simple/minimal). Arabic designs ₹500 se, full hand ₹1,500 se aur complete bridal package ₹5,000 se. Exact rate design ki detailing, size aur location par depend karta hai — photo bhejein to turant quote mil jayega.

Kya aap ghar par aakar mehndi lagati hain?

Haan. Poore Indore mein ghar par service available hai — Vijay Nagar, Palasia, Rau, Bhawarkuan, Sudama Nagar, MR 10 aur baaki areas. Aapko sirf ek chair aur thoda sa roshni ki jagah chahiye.

Bridal mehndi mein kitna time lagta hai?

Bridal (haath + pair) mein 2.5 se 4 ghante lag sakte hain, design ki detailing ke hisaab se. Isliye shaadi se ek din pehle ya subah ka time rakhna best rehta hai.

Colour gehra aayega?

Organic henna use hota hai, aur colour 24–48 ghante mein deepen hota hai. Maine aftercare ke simple tips bhi diye hain — garmi se door rehna, lemon-sugar seal, aur paani se bachna. Sahi care se colour kaafi gehra aata hai.

Bridal date kitne pehle book karni chahiye?

Normal dinon mein 3–4 hafte pehle. Shaadi season (Nov–Feb) mein 1–2 mahine pehle. Ek baar date confirm ho jaaye to main us din sirf aapke liye available rehti hoon.

Advance dena padta hai?

Bridal dates ke liye chhota advance liya jaata hai, taaki date block ho sake. Chhoti bookings ke liye mehndi ke baad payment — cash ya UPI.
